Rajesh Sirigireddy, MSHI
Executive Public Health Consultant
Digital Health & AI Strategist

Rajesh is a global public health leader with over 13 years of experience bridging data science, innovation, and implementation. He specializes in public health strategy, AI-powered analytics, and evidence-driven program design.

Throughout his career, Rajesh has guided organizations and innovators through the complete journey from concept to market — conducting feasibility studies, developing prototypes, leading pilot implementations, and supporting scaled deployment.

Rajesh combines rigorous research methodology with system-level strategic thinking and human-centered design to create solutions that are both innovative and practical.
